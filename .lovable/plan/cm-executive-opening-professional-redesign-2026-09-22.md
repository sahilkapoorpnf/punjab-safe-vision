# CM Executive Opening — Professional Redesign

## Scope

## Visual direction
- White government-report canvas on all nine new screens.
- Subtle, low-opacity Congress hand watermark on every executive screen using the supplied logo; no campaign-style slogans or dominant party branding.
- Restrained Himachal red, green, blue and charcoal accents; large bilingual Hindi-English headings; minimal supporting copy.
- Strong visual hierarchy suitable for presentation directly to the Chief Minister.

8. **Human journey** — authentic rehabilitation photograph plus a simple six-stage recovery pathway; no fabricated person record or outcome.
9. **Transition** — concise bilingual closing statement and proposed-solution identity, leading directly into the untouched product deck.
